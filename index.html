How to use mnemonics

  1. Make it vivid. The funnier, stranger, or more exaggerated the image, the stronger the memory. A ridiculous image beats a sensible one.
  2. Use all your senses. Imagine the sound, smell, texture, and motion of the scene, not just the visual.
  3. Link sound to meaning. Connect the Japanese sounds to an English word or image that sounds similar.
  4. Rehearse briefly and often. Say the mnemonic aloud 3–5 times over spaced intervals. Each recall strengthens the connection.
  5. Combine with SRS. Use the Flashcards tab to review words at expanding intervals. Mnemonics help encoding; SRS handles retention.
